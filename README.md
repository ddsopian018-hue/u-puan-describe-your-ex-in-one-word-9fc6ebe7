My ex in one word: **Subscription**.

The joke lands at a glance: an ex you kept paying for month after month — emotionally, financially, in attention — and only realised you could cancel it far too late. You finally hit unsubscribe, and yet the renewal emails keep coming: the late-night "u up?", the birthday message, the mutual-friend updates. It is a verdict on a *behaviour*, not a generic insult, and it reframes the whole relationship as a service nobody remembers signing up for. Everyone who has had an ex has felt the exact moment of cancelling something that should never have auto-renewed.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes `Subscription` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (or `./output/entry.txt` when the variable is unset).
